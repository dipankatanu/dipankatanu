## About Me

I am **Dr. Dipanka Tanu Sarmah**, a postdoctoral researcher at **RCSI (Ireland)** focusing on **multi-omics systems biology of Parkinson’s disease**.

Core areas of work and expertise:

- Spatiotemporal omics (CosMx/GeoMx spatial transcriptomics, temporal single-cell trajectories)  
- Multi-omics integration (RNA-seq, proteomics, phospho-proteomics, metabolomics)  
- Pipeline development in R, Python, and MATLAB for reproducible analysis  
- LLM + RAG workflows for ontology grouping, drug repurposing and knowledge mining  
- Mathematical and mechanistic modeling (tumor-immune dynamics, NRF2-KEAP1, Beclin1 autophagy)  
- Network science (graph analytics, GNNs, disease–drug–gene interaction inference)

My goal is to transform complex datasets into **interpretable biological models, predictive tools, and open-source software**.
